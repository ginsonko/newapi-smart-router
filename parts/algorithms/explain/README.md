# Explain

The planner and attempt lifecycle emit stable reason codes. Explanations are
derived from receipts and snapshots, not reconstructed from current state.

Users may see selected group, effective ratio, strategy, retry count, and why a
cheaper route was excluded. Administrators may additionally see physical route
identity, evidence age, recovery action, capacity domain, and reconciliation.
Neither role receives upstream credentials, raw URLs, private headers, or
another user's request content.
